## About Us

The **[JKUAT Social Robotics Lab](https://www.jkuatsocialroboticslab.com/)** is a research group focused on advancing intelligent systems that interact naturally with humans. We explore the intersection of robotics, artificial intelligence, natural language processing, and human–robot interaction (HRI).

### Our mission is to:

- Build socially-aware robotic systems
- Explore speech, vision, and multimodal interaction
- Develop AI-enabled behaviors for humanoid platforms
- Enable undergraduate and postgraduate research in robotics and AI
- Contribute open-source tools, datasets, and frameworks to the community

---

## Research Areas

We actively work on:

- **Human–Robot Interaction (HRI)**
- **Speech & Natural Language Understanding**
- **LLM integration for humanoid robots** (NAO, Pepper, etc.)
- **Computer Vision & Perception**
- **Robot Autonomy & Behavior Modeling**
- **Robot Control APIs & Middleware**
- **Social Robotics Applications** (education, healthcare, companionship and many more..)
